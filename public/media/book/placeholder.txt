The room smelled of old paper and something faintly electrical.

She picked up the book from the counter, its spine cracked and faded. The pages fell open to a passage marked in pencil, words underlined with a trembling hand:

"What you are looking for is not here. It was never here. But you knew that already, didn't you?"

The ink seemed to shift in the dim light.

She turned the page, but there was nothing more.
