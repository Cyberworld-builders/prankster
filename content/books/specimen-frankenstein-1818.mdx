## Two books with one name

*Frankenstein; or, The Modern Prometheus* came out in London on 1 January 1818, in three volumes, with no author's name on it. The version most people read today is the revised edition of 1831, which carries Mary Shelley's own introduction about how the story started.

> I busied myself to think of a story,—a story to rival those which had excited us to this task.

That introduction places the start of the book at the Villa Diodati in the summer of 1816, during a ghost-story contest with Lord Byron, John Polidori and Percy Shelley.
